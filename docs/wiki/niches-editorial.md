# Niches and editorial policy

Niche profiles live in config/niches/. They define topic coverage, freshness expectations, preferred source types and editorial guardrails. Keep the niche separate from language and tone so one engine can create multiple editions without duplicating code.

## Included niche profiles
AI; consumer technology; sports; entertainment; science; health; finance; crypto; photography; automotive; gaming; business; education; environment.

## Suggested source priority
1. Primary-source material: official announcements, regulatory filings, league/agency notices or published research.
2. Reputable reporting with clear attribution.
3. Other sources only when the configured source-quality policy allows them.

Source authority is contextual. A company is authoritative about what it announced, but its performance claim is not automatically independently verified.

## Higher-risk topics
- **Health:** preserve study design and limitations; no personalized diagnosis, treatment or dosage recommendations. Hold consequential claims for review.
- **Finance:** distinguish reporting from investment advice; preserve date, currency and market context.
- **Crypto:** never invent live price data; timestamp market information and label unverified allegations.
- **Cybersecurity:** avoid unsupported exploit claims and unverified attribution.
- **Sports:** verify team, competition, date, result and player status; do not invent live scores.
- **Entertainment:** distinguish rumours from confirmed releases and avoid unsupported claims about identifiable people.
- **Science/environment:** distinguish preprints, peer-reviewed evidence, observations and projections.

## Editorial output principles
Generate original articles in the chosen language from a verified evidence record. Do not mechanically translate or imitate the publisher's paragraph structure. Preserve attribution and uncertainty; do not invent quotes, values, deadlines or local impacts.
